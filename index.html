Before you visit

Common questions.

Anything else, ask at the clinic when you come in — both doctors are happy to talk it through.

Find your nearest clinic
Do I need an appointment?
No. Both clinics run on a walk-in basis during morning and evening sessions.
Which doctor should I see?
Come in and describe the complaint — we'll point you to the right one. As a rough guide: children and long-standing allergies tend to go to Dr. Rajashri Patil, while digestive, joint and chronic conditions usually go to Dr. Narendra Patil.
Are the two clinics open at the same times?
Yes — both run 9:30 AM to 2:00 PM and 5:30 PM to 10:00 PM, every day. Go to whichever is closer to you.
Can Ayurvedic or homeopathic treatment run alongside my existing medicines?
In most cases, yes — but bring your current prescriptions with you so they can be checked first. Never stop a prescribed medicine without discussing it at the visit.
Do you treat children and infants?
Yes, from newborns upward — routine weight and growth checks, vaccination guidance, and treatment for the usual childhood illnesses.
What should I bring to a first visit?
Any previous prescriptions, recent test reports, and a list of medicines you're currently taking. For children, the immunisation card if you have it.

Not feeling right? Just walk in.

No appointment, no forms to fill in first. Both clinics are open twice a day, seven days a week — come to whichever is closer.
